LEARNING & ARCHITECTURAL EXPERIENCE

DEVELOPER / LEAD DESIGNER: Sabharish K. // MODULE: Member 5 — Final Accusation, Verdict Reveal, Evidence Synthesis & Scoring Engine

Developing Module 5 for ¿Quién Fue? provided valuable hands-on experience in building a complex, accessible state machine entirely in vanilla JavaScript without the crutch of heavy external frameworks. My primary architectural challenge was ensuring that the final reveal served as the dramatic narrative climax of the entire team's project while maintaining a strict, self-contained footprint that can interface seamlessly with Modules 1 through 4 via URL parameters, localStorage, or global window objects.

Balancing bilingual user experience was another major focus. Rather than forcing an English-speaking student through an impenetrable wall of Spanish or rendering the language requirement superficial, I structured the interface with English as the primary communicative vehicle and embedded forensic Spanish terminology naturally into authentic institutional stamps, suspect files, and an interactive 30-word glossary. Crafting the keyboard-accessible radiogroup and ensuring WCAG AA contrast against our cinematic near-black palette reinforced how vital thoughtful semantic HTML is when creating immersive digital learning experiences.
